About COVID-19

Coronavirus disease (COVID-19) is an infectious disease caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus. Most people infected with the virus experience mild to moderate respiratory illness and recover without requiring special treatment.

Common Symptoms

🤒 Fever

High body temperature is one of the primary symptoms reported.

😷 Dry Cough

Persistent dry coughing without phlegm production.

😴 Fatigue

Feeling unusually tired or weak throughout the day.

👅 Loss of Taste/Smell

Sudden loss or change in your sense of taste or smell.

Preventative Measures

Wear a Mask

Wear well-fitted masks in crowded indoor spaces.

Wash Hands

Clean hands regularly with soap and water or alcohol-based sanitizer.

Get Vaccinated

Keep your vaccinations and booster doses up to date.

Social Distance

Maintain safe physical distance from individuals displaying symptoms.
